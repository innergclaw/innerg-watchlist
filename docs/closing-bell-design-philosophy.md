# Closing Bell design philosophy

The closing graphic should feel like a market desk, not a promotional flyer. A deep charcoal field keeps attention on the numbers. One sharp lime rule connects the visual to INNERG Market Pulse without turning the page into decoration.

The composition uses two equal columns because the report has one clear comparison: strength against weakness. Gainers sit on the left and losers sit on the right. The matching structure helps a reader scan rank, ticker, price, and change without learning two layouts.

Typography carries the hierarchy. A large, direct headline names the report. Monospaced figures keep prices and percentages aligned. Company names stay quiet so tickers and market movement remain the first read.

Color has a narrow job. Lime marks positive movement. Soft red marks negative movement. Warm white and gray support labels, borders, and context. No extra chart shapes or decorative marks compete with the closing data.

The final image is built at 1600 by 900 pixels. It works as a website card, email snapshot, and landscape social asset. The bottom note states the method, removes crypto from the closing comparison, and keeps the research-only boundary visible.
