How to read the numbers

1 day compares the latest price with the prior session close. 1 week and 30 days compare it with the available close on or before that date. These are price changes, not returns including dividends.

Choose 1D, 1W, or 30D on any chart. Drag across the line or move the slider to see recorded prices. Arrow keys move one sample; Home and End jump to the first and last. 1D shows five-minute samples from the latest regular stock session, or the latest 24 hours for crypto. Mutual funds may not have intraday data. 1W and 30D show daily samples within the last 7 or 30 calendar days.

Daily dates use UTC, matching the provider's crypto day boundaries. Intraday times use Eastern time and identify the start of each five-minute bar; its price is that bar's recorded closing value. The latest bar may still be forming. Lines connect samples; they do not supply prices between them. Each chart uses its own scale. Chart windows and return comparisons can start on different dates. Open an asset's ranges for daily and 52-week highs and lows.

Prices can be delayed. Snapshot time is when this page's data was collected, not the time of each trade. Unavailable data is marked, never filled with an estimate.
